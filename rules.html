Community Standards

18+ Only

Pinnacle SMP is an 18+ server. You must be 18 years old or older to join.

  • Members must be 18 or older.
  • This applies to all applicants and players.
  • Age requirements help keep the server mature and community-focused.

Note: Players who do not meet the age requirement are not eligible to join.

Staff may use discretion when handling situations that are not directly covered here. When in doubt, ask an Admin or Founding Member.
