PLUGIN GUIDE

Pinnacle AFK

Player-facing guidance for the plugins used on Pinnacle SMP.

WIKI LAST REVIEWED: SEPTEMBER 1, 2026Plugin features, commands, and server permissions may change after updates. This guide may not always reflect the newest server version immediately.
UTILITY

Pinnacle AFK provides manual and optional automatic AFK status, a complete movement and action lock, visible AFK markers, announcements, and delayed damage protection.

PLAYER COMMAND Going AFK

Use /afk when you need to step away from the game. The same command returns you from AFK mode.

Command

CommandWhat it does
/afkToggles your AFK status on or off.

ADMIN ONLY AFK Management Commands

CommandWhat it does
/pafk reloadValidates and reloads the PinnacleAFK configuration.
/pafk listLists online players who are currently AFK.
/pafk status <player>Checks an online player's AFK status.
/pafk remove <player>Removes AFK status from an online player.

What Happens While AFK

  • [AFK] is shown with your name in the player list and above your character.
  • Your position and view direction are locked, and normal gameplay actions such as combat, block use, inventories, item movement, projectiles, teleports, and portals are blocked.
  • The server announces when you go AFK and when you return.
  • After a short delay, you become protected from normal damage while still AFK.
  • Your existing rank, team, tab-list formatting, and team mechanics remain intact and return normally when AFK mode ends.
  • AFK status is cleared safely on death, disconnect, or an administrator removal.

Recommended Use

  1. Move to a safe location before going AFK.
  2. Run /afk and wait for the protection confirmation.
  3. When you return, run /afk again before trying to move.

Important Notes

Protection is delayed. Do not assume you are immediately invincible the moment you type /afk.
Automatic AFK detection is optional and may be disabled by the server. When you enter AFK manually, use /afk again to return; ordinary movement does not clear the lock.
