Season 12 Overview

STARTED APRIL 2026 • LONG-TERM SURVIVAL • COMMUNITY-BUILT WORLD
APR 2026

Season Start

Season 12 began with a fresh world and a renewed focus on long-term building, player-driven projects, and organic progression.

LAUNCH

Vanilla First

The season launched as a pure vanilla survival experience so early progression could remain clean and traditional.

JUN 2026

Paper 26.2

The server later moved back to Paper and updated to Paper 26.2: Chaos Cubed, adding performance support and custom community tools without abandoning the vanilla-first philosophy.

World Setup

SettingSeason 12 Detail
GameplaySurvival, whitelisted, community-focused, and Vanilla+.
World BorderApproximately 4,000 blocks across. The border encourages interaction, shared infrastructure, and a connected community layout.
Spawn TownBuilt by players rather than pre-built. Roads, starter buildings, communal areas, and shops develop naturally.
Hosting24/7 server hosted in the eastern United States.
World ResetsNo fixed reset schedule. A reset requires a server-wide vote with at least 90% agreement.

Season Philosophy

  • Close-proximity gameplay and more opportunities for player interaction
  • Player-built progression instead of heavily predefined systems
  • Technical improvements that support the world without overpowering it
  • Community input on major decisions such as world-border changes
  • A world intended to develop and remain meaningful over time
The world border is not necessarily permanent. Expansion or removal may be decided through a community vote later in the season.
