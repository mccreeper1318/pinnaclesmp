About Pinnacle SMP

Where It Began

Pinnacle SMP traces its history to 2012, when McCreeper1318 began hosting a public Minecraft server. Griefing led the server to become whitelisted, but not before McCreeper1318 met Piff and Jeff.

McCreeper1318, Piff, and Jeff began the first season as a purely vanilla, Mindcrack-inspired survival server. JohnnyKilroy joined soon afterward and helped launch Season 2 after Piff left.

COMMUNITY FIRST • SERVER SECOND

How the Server Evolved

Across many years and seasons, Pinnacle grew into its current community. In 2025, the server adopted a Vanilla+ approach for the first time: Paper and carefully chosen plugins support performance, safety, communication, and quality of life while preserving Minecraft’s core survival experience.

Where Pinnacle Is Going

Pinnacle is currently in Season 12, which began in April 2026. There are no plans to begin Season 13 soon. The focus remains on long-term progression, community trust, shared infrastructure, events, and giving players time to find their place in the world.

Membership: The community has more than 20 members in varying states of activity. Membership applications are temporarily closed while the current community settles into Season 12.
