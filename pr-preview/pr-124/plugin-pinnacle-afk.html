Pinnacle AFK

WIKI LAST REVIEWED: AUGUST 5, 2026Plugin features, commands, and server permissions may change after updates. This guide may not always reflect the newest server version immediately.
UTILITY

Pinnacle AFK provides a clear manual AFK status, movement lock, visible AFK markers, announcements, and delayed damage protection.

PLAYER COMMAND Going AFK

Use /afk when you need to step away from the game. The same command returns you from AFK mode.

Command

CommandWhat it does
/afkToggles your AFK status on or off.

What Happens While AFK

  • [AFK] is shown with your name in the player list and above your character.
  • Your movement is locked until you use /afk again.
  • The server announces when you go AFK and when you return.
  • After a short delay, you become protected from normal damage while still AFK.
  • Your existing rank or team prefix is restored when AFK mode ends.

Recommended Use

  1. Move to a safe location before going AFK.
  2. Run /afk and wait for the protection confirmation.
  3. When you return, run /afk again before trying to move.

Important Notes

Protection is delayed. Do not assume you are immediately invincible the moment you type /afk.
The command is a manual toggle. The plugin does not require you to move around to clear AFK status; use /afk again.
