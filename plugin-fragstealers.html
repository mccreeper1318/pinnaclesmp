FragStealers

WIKI LAST REVIEWED: AUGUST 5, 2026Plugin features, commands, and server permissions may change after updates. This guide may not always reflect the newest server version immediately.
SECURITY, SHOPS & MAIL

FragStealers combines secure containers, protected player shops, virtual mailboxes, and staff recovery tools.

PLAYER FEATURES Locks, Shops, and Mailboxes

FragStealers protects supported containers and adds player-run shops and private virtual mailboxes. It is designed to reduce theft and griefing while keeping trading and item delivery simple.

Protected Storage

  1. Attach a sign to an empty chest, trapped chest, double chest, or barrel.
  2. Put [fs] on the first line.
  3. The sign changes to [protected] and shows the owner's name.
  • Only the owner can normally open the container or remove/edit its protection sign.
  • The protected container cannot be broken until the protection sign is removed.
  • Remove the sign first when you want to move or dismantle your own protected container.

Player Shops

  1. Attach a sign to an empty supported container.
  2. Put [fs shop] on the first line.
  3. Right-click the sign and choose Setup Shop.
  4. Select the item and quantity being sold, then the payment item and quantity.
  5. Stock the physical container with the sale item.

Customers right-click the shop sign to use the purchase interface. Owners receive the management interface and can collect stored payments. Sale quantities follow the item's actual maximum stack size.

Mailboxes

  1. Attach a sign to an empty supported container.
  2. Put [fs mail] on the first line.
  3. Leave the second line blank to create the mailbox for yourself.
  • Other players can deposit items into available mailbox slots but cannot take existing mail.
  • The owner can open pickup mode to collect the real virtual inventory.
  • Mail is stored virtually rather than inside the physical chest or barrel.
  • Owners receive a You've Got Mail! notice when appropriate.
  • A barrel or single chest provides 27 mailbox slots; a double chest provides 54.

Normal Player Commands

No command is required for ordinary locks, shops, or mailboxes. Players create and use them through signs and right-click interfaces.

ADMIN ONLY Master Key and Recovery Tools

The Master Key is an unbreakable custom wooden axe used by authorized staff to inspect or recover protected systems. Holding the item alone is not enough; the staff permission is also required.

CommandWhat it does
/fs give masterkeyGives the command user a FragStealers Master Key.
/fs give masterkey <player>Gives a Master Key to another player.
/fs reloadReloads FragStealers without deleting saved protections.

Creating a Mailbox for Another Player

While holding an authorized Master Key, staff can place a mailbox sign with [fs mail] on line 1 and the target player's name on line 2. The mailbox belongs to the target player exactly as though they created it.

Authorized staff can open protected storage and management interfaces and can remove protection signs for recovery. A Master Key does not directly break a container that is still protected.

Important Notes

Physical versus virtual storage: Ordinary locks and shop stock use the actual container inventory. Mailbox contents and shop payments are stored separately by the plugin.
Removing a shop: Remove the shop sign only when you intend to dismantle the shop. Stored payments are returned to the person authorized to remove it, and overflow items can drop nearby.
Double chests: Treat the full double chest as one protected system. Do not try to alter one half to bypass its sign.
